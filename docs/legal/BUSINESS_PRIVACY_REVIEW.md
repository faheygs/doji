## Scope

This notice explains personal information used for business accounts, applications, review and workspace access at business.dojipro.com. Business contact information can still be personal information, including a sole proprietor's name or address. Personal use of the Doji member app is covered by its separate privacy notice. Business registration does not create a member profile or make application details public.

We receive information directly from you, generate account and review records while providing the service, and receive authentication, security and email-delivery results from service providers. Acknowledging this notice does not waive your rights or authorize unrelated uses of your information.

## Information and purposes

- Account information: email, display name, authentication records, accepted terms and acknowledged privacy-notice versions and timestamps, password credentials handled by our authentication provider, and enrolled multi-factor authentication information. Used to establish and secure access, document the account agreement, verify email and support recovery.
- Application information: legal and brand names, business website, country, representative name and role, industry and intended use of Doji. The initial application does not ask for a street address; an address included in an earlier submitted record may remain in that historical record until reviewed under the retention or privacy-request process. Used to identify the business, assess an application and administer an approved relationship.
- Review records: submitted versions, submission times, accepted terms and acknowledged privacy versions, status, reviewer decisions, responses and internal review notes. Used to review consistently, explain decisions and investigate mistakes or misuse. Internal notes are restricted to authorized staff.
- Service and security information: request and authentication events, device/browser and network information processed by our infrastructure providers, email delivery records, and rate-limit counters. Used to operate the service, prevent automated abuse and investigate failures. Doji's dedicated signup limiter uses shared hashed address buckets rather than storing raw email addresses in its limiter records; account and email systems still process your email.
- Support correspondence: information you choose to send when asking for help. Used to address the request and verify authority where necessary.

Required form fields are needed to process the application. You can choose not to provide them, but we may then be unable to review it or provide access. Do not include identity documents, payment data, customer records or sensitive personal information in free-text fields. We will not ask you to send passwords or authenticator codes to support.

## Who receives information

Authorized Doji staff review applications and handle support. We use Supabase for authentication and database services, Cloudflare for hosting and bot protection, and Resend for verification and recovery emails. These providers process the information needed for those functions. Providers operating our support mailbox also process support correspondence. We do not make business applications publicly searchable.

Cloudflare Turnstile evaluates signals such as IP address, browser characteristics and the site being accessed to distinguish legitimate visitors from automated abuse. Cloudflare also describes its own processing to improve bot detection. See its [Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/) for those practices and rights contacts. Doji's security check does not send application details or passwords to Turnstile.

We may disclose information when legally required, to respond to valid legal process or when reasonably necessary to address fraud, threats or unauthorized access, subject to applicable law.

## Browser storage and messages

The business account client keeps access tokens in page memory. Reloading can require signing in again. The site stores a theme preference locally. Infrastructure and security services may also process browser information as described above. Browser storage and security processing are not permission to subscribe you to marketing.

Verification and recovery emails are service messages, not marketing subscriptions. Creating an account or submitting an application does not subscribe you to a marketing list. The onboarding implementation does not enable campaign billing or paid realtime features.

## Retention and closure

We retain information for the time needed to operate the account, review applications, maintain necessary security and decision records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. We consider the information's sensitivity, the reason for keeping it, applicable duties and whether a less identifying record will suffice.

Submitted application versions and review history are separate from your sign-in account. Editing a draft, withdrawing an application or closing a sign-in account does not itself erase those records. A correction may be recorded alongside an earlier submission to preserve an accurate history. This does not remove any deletion or correction rights you have under applicable law.

Review requirement: this section is not ready for publication until the category-specific retention and deletion procedure in BUSINESS_LEGAL_REVIEW.md is approved and tested, including submitted records, support correspondence and backups. No automatic deletion period is promised in this draft.

To request closure, withdrawal, access, correction or deletion, contact support@dojipro.com. Identify the account and explain your request; do not send a password, verification code or identity document in ordinary email. We may need proportionate verification of your identity and, for requests affecting the business account, your authority. We will explain any additional information needed. Deletion may be limited where applicable law permits or requires retention, and retained information will be restricted to the justified purpose. Request handling must be operationally verified before this draft is published.
