# Business privacy operations — initial US launch

Owner: Gavin Fahey. Intake and appeals: support@dojipro.com, the existing Cloudflare-to-Google mailbox. This is the operational implementation of the approved criteria-based notice, not a new fixed retention promise or a legal opinion. Initial admission is capped at ten accounts; no automated erasure or paid service is enabled.

## Intake, verification and fulfillment

Record each request in the restricted business privacy queue. Verify the exact business account and representative authority before disclosing, correcting or closing anything. Email access or public business facts alone must not bypass MFA. Do not collect identity documents or credentials in ordinary email. Record only a verification reference and minimal reason in the case. Determine the applicable deadline from the request and applicable law; promptly acknowledge it and escalate uncertainty to the operator. A generic fixed statutory deadline is not assumed for every request.

Use the released employee-MFA-only detail and correction readers. Corrections target the current draft revision with a stable command key; submitted snapshots remain historical evidence. Export only the subject's authorized data, excluding unrelated people and internal security material. Prefer secure delivery after verification, not an unsolicited email attachment. The current correction command does not change the Auth email address.

Closure disables only the business account through its audited workflow. Erasure requires a verified exact target, no active hold, separately authorized execution and the tested business erasure orchestration. Check the resumable receipt after provider failures; never treat an ambiguous failure as permission for a second target or delete a member identity. The browser has no erasure executor. Maintain the primary-database result separately from Auth, mailbox, email-provider and backup handling; do not mark all copies erased based on one command. See the business privacy implementation/tests and release record for exact contracts.

## Category-specific retention review

At each closure, withdrawal, declined application, privacy request or security incident, the operator reviews the following categories. Also review the capped launch's inactive accounts before renewing its admission window. This is a manual responsibility, not a new scheduled job.

| Category | Justification and disposal decision |
| --- | --- |
| Unverified account and abandoned current draft | Keep only while verification/application is actively needed. If abandoned and no documented justified hold remains, use the verified closure/erasure process. Do not roll inactive data into indefinite retention. |
| Approved account/application | Keep information needed for the active relationship. Review at closure; remove unnecessary identifying details while retaining only justified evidence. |
| Submitted versions, decisions and agreement evidence | Assess disputes, legal duties and security need separately from active access. A need for a minimal decision/consent record does not justify retaining all free text. Record reason and next review condition for any retained data. |
| Security and hashed limiter records | Use only for operation and abuse control. Fixed buckets store no raw email. Provider authentication/security logs follow provider controls; do not promise immediate per-record deletion where unavailable. |
| Support and service emails | Review the exact account's correspondence in the operator mailbox and delivery provider. Remove unnecessary content and trash where authorized; retain only justified case evidence. Never purge unrelated mail. |
| Provider backups and exports | Record where relevant copies exist and provider expiration/restore handling when fulfilling a request. Restrict retained backups from routine use. If restoring, reapply completed erasures before returning restored data to service. Do not promise a provider backup deadline that has not been verified. |

A retention hold needs a reason, restricted scope and review condition. Releasing a hold does not execute deletion by itself. Notify the requester of any permitted limitation, its reason, and the support appeal route. No fixed 30/90/180-day periods from the earlier proposal were adopted.

## Account recovery and incidents

Use an existing factor first. If all factors are lost, keep access restricted pending independent account/authority verification and a separately authorized tested recovery operation. There is no staff MFA-reset shortcut in this release. Never request passwords, TOTP seeds or verification codes by email.

For suspected exposure, contain the specific business entry point/account, preserve minimal restricted evidence, identify affected records/providers and assess required notices. Do not revoke member sessions or disable shared authentication as a routine business response. The operator handles escalation; this release purchases no services.

The written inventory and limited retention approach follow the [FTC data-security guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business). It does not set our retention periods or certify compliance. Country scope, city-only contact details and approved draft choices are documented in the legal review. Reassess before expanding internationally or enabling marketing or paid campaigns.
