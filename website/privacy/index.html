Privacy Policy

Your activity is social. Your data deserves care.

This policy explains what Doji collects, why we use it, who helps us operate the service, and the choices you have.

Effective August 11, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect account information such as your email address, username, optional display name, profile photo, and bio. We also collect content and interactions you choose to share, including photos, videos, captions, poll responses, comments, reactions, challenge suggestions, reports, and blocks.

To operate the product, we process friendships, challenge eligibility and participation, streaks, XP, Sparks, badges, shop ownership, equipped cosmetics, notification preferences, and durable notification history. We also receive device push tokens and limited device, app-version, network, security, timestamp, and diagnostic information.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate your account, deliver daily challenges and notifications, show authorized social content, calculate streaks and rewards, synchronize realtime activity, personalize appearance, prevent abuse, moderate reported content, troubleshoot reliability, provide support, and improve Doji.

3. Content and social visibility

Content you submit may be visible to friends or the wider Doji community according to the audience shown in the app. Your username, optional display name, avatar, equipped frame and title, public activity statistics, and earned badges may be visible to other users.

Poll and Would You Rather results may use community-wide totals. Social alerts about participation, reactions, and comments are limited to the applicable friends and content owners.

4. Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server functions; Cloudflare for durable orchestration, event delivery, and website hosting; Ably for realtime messaging; Expo for mobile builds and push delivery; Sentry for production error diagnostics; Apple for iOS distribution and optional Apple sign-in; and Google for optional Google sign-in. These providers process information only to provide their services to us and under their own privacy and security commitments.

We do not sell personal information or use your content for targeted advertising.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Doji stores a device push token and uses it for the categories you enable. Push providers and operating systems cannot guarantee display. You can change notification categories in Doji and system-level permission in your device settings.

6. Safety and moderation

Reports and blocks may include the relevant account, content, reason, and timestamps so we can investigate abuse, enforce our rules, and protect users. We may preserve limited safety records when reasonably necessary to prevent repeated abuse or comply with law.

7. Retention and account deletion

We retain account information while your account is active and other information only as reasonably necessary to provide and secure the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. You can permanently delete your account in the app from Settings. You may also follow the instructions on our account deletion page.

Deletion removes or de-identifies account data subject to reasonable backup, legal, fraud-prevention, and safety retention.

8. Your choices

You can edit profile information, manage notification preferences, remove friends, block users, report content, clear notification history, and delete your account. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion assistance.

9. Security

We use safeguards designed to protect information, including authenticated access, database authorization policies, scoped storage, encrypted network transport, and limited service credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Doji is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information in violation of this policy.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Doji changes. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice when required. Privacy questions or requests may be sent to support@dojipro.com.
