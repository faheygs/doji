Child Safety Standards

Child exploitation has no place on Doji.

These standards explain how Doji prevents, reports, and responds to child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Effective September 9, 2026

Zero-tolerance standard

Doji prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM); grooming or sexual solicitation of minors; sextortion; trafficking; sexualization of a child; sharing intimate imagery of a minor; and links, instructions, or attempts to obtain, create, distribute, or normalize such material. This rule applies to profiles, photos, videos, captions, comments, reactions, messages, challenge suggestions, and any other activity on Doji.

Age requirement and prevention

Doji is for people age 13 and older. We require an age-eligibility declaration during signup and may restrict or remove accounts when we learn that the requirement is not met. We use account controls, scoped visibility, reporting, blocking, moderation records, and enforcement tools to reduce abuse and protect users.

How to report

Use the report action on a post, comment, or member profile to send the relevant account, content, reason, and timestamp to Doji's moderation team. Use Block for immediate separation. For a child-safety concern that cannot be reported in the app, email support@dojipro.com. Do not email or forward suspected CSAM.

Our response

We review reports, preserve relevant safety records where lawful, remove prohibited content, and restrict or permanently disable accounts when warranted. When we become aware of apparent CSAM or child exploitation, we report it to the appropriate authority as required by law and cooperate with lawful investigations. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Child-safety contact

Google Play and other authorized safety partners may contact Doji's designated child-safety point of contact at support@dojipro.com.
