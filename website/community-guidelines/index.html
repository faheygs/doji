Community Guidelines

Make it fun. Never make it unsafe.

Doji is built for real people sharing real moments. These rules apply to profiles, posts, photos, videos, poll answers, comments, reactions, and challenge suggestions.

Effective August 11, 2026

Respect people

Do not harass, threaten, bully, shame, stalk, dox, or target another person. Hate speech or attacks based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender, sexual orientation, or another protected characteristic are not allowed.

Keep content appropriate

Do not post nudity, sexually explicit material, graphic violence, cruelty, self-harm encouragement, illegal goods, dangerous instructions, or content that exploits or endangers minors. Do not upload intimate or private media without the subject's permission.

Be authentic

Do not impersonate people or organizations, spread scams, manipulate votes or reactions, falsify challenge completion, send spam, or coordinate abuse. Share only content you have permission to use.

Report and block

Use the report action on objectionable content so our moderation team receives the account, content, and reason needed to investigate. Use Block when you need immediate distance: the user and their content are removed from your experience right away. You can use both controls when you want immediate separation and a moderation review.

What happens after a report

We review objectionable-content reports within 24 hours. Depending on severity and history, we may remove content, warn or restrict an account, suspend access, or permanently eject the responsible user. Serious safety or legal threats may be escalated to the appropriate authorities.

Appeals and urgent concerns

If you believe we made a mistake, or if a concern cannot be reported in the app, email support@dojipro.com. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
