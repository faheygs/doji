Doji Support

Let's get you back in.

Email support@dojipro.com with your username and a short description of what happened. Never send a password, sign-in code, or private photo.

Challenge or notification issue

Include the approximate time, your timezone, the challenge title, and whether Doji was open, in the background, or closed. A push alert helps you know the Doji is live, but the app always checks the server for the authoritative participation window.

Posts, comments, or reactions

If activity is not updating, confirm you have a connection and reopen the relevant feed or thread. If the committed activity still does not appear, send us the usernames involved and the approximate time. Do not repeatedly submit the same action.

Report or block someone

Open the relevant post, comment, or member profile and use its action menu. Report sends the content to moderation. Block removes the account and its content from your experience immediately. Use both controls when you want immediate separation and a moderation review. We review objectionable-content reports within 24 hours.

Notifications

Open Settings, then Notification settings to choose your alert categories. Also confirm Doji notifications are enabled in iPhone Settings. Clearing an in-app notification removes it from your Doji account across devices and reinstalls.

Delete your account

Open Profile, Settings, then Delete account. For more detail or if you cannot access the app, use our account deletion instructions.

Privacy or security concern

Email support@dojipro.com with enough detail for us to investigate. Do not include passwords, sign-in codes, or sensitive private media.
