Account deletion

You can leave Doji at any time.

Account deletion is permanent. These instructions explain how to delete from the app or request help when you cannot sign in.

Delete in the Doji app

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm deletion.

Doji signs you out when deletion finishes.

If you cannot access the app

Email support@dojipro.com from the email address associated with your Doji account. Include your username. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request, but we will never ask for your password or sign-in code.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes your authentication account, profile, personal media, challenge participation, posts, comments, reactions, friendships, notification history, shop ownership, rewards, and other personal account records, or de-identifies them where needed to preserve another user's legitimate conversation or aggregate result.

Limited retention

Backups and security logs may retain limited information for a short period before routine deletion. We may retain information when required by law, needed to resolve disputes, or reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, or repeated safety violations.
