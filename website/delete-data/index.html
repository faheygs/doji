Data controls

Delete specific data without deleting your account.

Use Doji's in-app controls for common changes, or contact support for help with a specific deletion request.

Delete a comment

Open the post's comments, use the action menu on your own comment, and choose Delete. The comment and its associated likes are removed.

Clear notification history

Open Notifications and choose Clear notifications. This clears your Doji notification-center history across your devices.

Change profile data

Open Profile, Settings, then Edit profile to replace or remove editable profile information such as your display name, bio, and profile photo.

Request removal of other specific data

Email support@dojipro.com from the email address associated with your account. Include your username and identify the content or data you want removed. We may ask you to verify account ownership, but we will never ask for your password or sign-in code.

We remove or de-identify the requested data when we can verify the request, subject to limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and safety enforcement. Operational backups may retain protected copies for a limited period before scheduled deletion.

Delete everything

To permanently delete your account and associated data, follow the account deletion instructions.
